# Chapter 4 — Op straat

Original: book pp. 58–67 (10 pages) → redesign: 15 pages.

## Content
Everything carried over: 16 dialogue turns, 59 vocabulary entries, 10 exercises,
the four reference cards (hoe het gaat, zullen, afspraak maken, samen iets doen)
with their ++ / + / +– / – and + / – reaction lists, the two Tekst boxes
(Talencentrum e-mail and address/opening hours), 29 e/ee words, Cultuur,
In de praktijk, Reflectie. Answers from Bijlage 8 (pp. 310–311): Opdracht 1, 3, 6, 8.

## Decisions
- **Bert is the waiter from chapter 3** ("De ober, Bert"), so he keeps the `ober`
  voice. **Hans is new**: Simon, picked by ear from five clear candidates
  (-21 to -26 dB above 8 kHz, all word-perfect).
- **Opdracht 9 has no audio**: like ch2's Opdracht 15 and ch3's Opdracht 12, its
  e/ee words are never printed. The 6 | 2 grid is kept; the icon is inert.
- **The shading in 4.3's "vertellen" card is not reproduced.** The scan shows a
  grey band behind *heel goed … niet zo (goed)* but nothing explains it, and in
  greyscale it may be a colour highlight that is now lost. Worth a spot-check
  against p. 61.
- **The Tekst page has no illustration.** A university building was generated for
  it, but the two text boxes plus Opdracht 8 fill the page and the image ran into
  the footer; it was dropped rather than shrunk to a sliver.
- **Opdracht 8's answer ("Donderdag") is written on its line**: answers.js learned
  the write-in-line shape for it.
- De/het drill: `het gaat wel` and `de groeten doen aan` are excluded as not nouns.
